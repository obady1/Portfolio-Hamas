About

Hamas
Islamic Resistance Movement

is a Palestinian organization founded in 1987 during the First Intifada. It combines political, social, and military activities and advocates for Palestinian rights. Hamas won the 2006 Palestinian legislative elections and has governed the Gaza Strip since 2007. Its military wing is known as the Izz ad-Din al-Qassam Brigades. International views differ, with some countries designating it as a terrorist organization and others viewing it as a resistance movement. Hamas remains a major actor in the Israeli–Palestinian conflict.

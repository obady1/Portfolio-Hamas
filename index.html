About

Hamas
Islamic Resistance Movement

"Hamas was founded in 1987 as a Palestinian organization that combines political, social, and military activities. The movement won the 2006 elections and has governed the Gaza Strip since 2007, with a military wing known as the Al-Qassam Brigades. The movement sparks international controversy, divided between those who designate it as a terrorist organization and others who view it as a resistance movement, and it remains a key player in the Israeli–Palestinian conflict."

Since 2007, Hamas has governed the Gaza Strip, managing political, civil, and security affairs under a blockade. It controls local institutions and internal security forces, while its military wing, the Izz ad-Din al-Qassam Brigades, oversees operations against Israel. Combining governmental administration with military activity, Hamas remains the dominant power in the territory.
